To imagine a cat's "inner hum" is to step away from the physics of the vibration and into the phenomenology of the sensation. If we strip away the performance—the rubbing against legs, the demand for food, the display of affection—what remains is a purely internal, somatic experience.

Here is what a cat likely notices in that moment of honest purring:

### 1. The Resonance of the Self
The most immediate sensation is not sound, but **vibration**. It is a tactile feedback loop. The cat feels the vibration originate in the larynx and spread instantly through the hyoid bone, down the trachea, and into the ribcage.
*   **The Notice:** It feels like the cat is holding a tuning fork against its own chest. The boundary between "inside" and "outside" blurs. The cat doesn't just hear the purr; it *is* the purr. It is a sensation of being whole, of the body acting as a single, resonant instrument.
