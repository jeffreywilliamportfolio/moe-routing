    *   **Key Concepts:** Feedback, Stability, Complexity, "Freezing" (over-constraint/lack of adaptability).

2.  **Deconstruct "Stabilize" vs. "Freeze":**
    *   *Stabilize:* Keeping the system within desired bounds, correcting deviations, maintaining equilibrium despite disturbances.
    *   *Freeze:* Making the system rigid, unresponsive, or stuck in a local optimum where it can't adapt to new conditions. This often happens with too much gain or too slow response.
    *   *The Tension:* Too little feedback = instability (wandering off). Too much feedback = over-correction (oscillation) or rigidity (stuck). The goal is *adaptive stability*.
